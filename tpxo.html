Global Harmonic Validation vs. TPXO9

Tidal constituents extracted from harmonicAnalysis.nc are compared against TPXO9 atlas amplitudes and phases at every ocean cell. Error metric: complex RMSE

E𝑐 = √[ ½(Aₘ² + Aₜ²) − Aₘ Aₜ cos(φₘ − φₜ) ]

Global: depth > 20 m. Deep: depth ≥ 1000 m, |lat| < 66°. Shallow: 20 – 1000 m, |lat| < 66°. RMSE is area-weighted. Green = best in column; red = worst.
